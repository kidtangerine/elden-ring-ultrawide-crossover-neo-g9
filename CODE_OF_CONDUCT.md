# Community code of conduct

This project welcomes respectful compatibility reports, testing, fixes, and
documentation improvements from players of every experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient, and specific.
- Respect different hardware, language, and accessibility needs.
- Share reproducible evidence without distributing copyrighted game files or
  private Steam/CrossOver data.
- Accept technical disagreement without personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
misinformation, piracy requests, or cheating guidance are not welcome.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed
to protect the community. Report sensitive conduct concerns privately to the
maintainer through GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting or account contact
channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially and promptly as practical.
